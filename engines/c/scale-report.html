logos-sort — time against n

Drag on a chart to zoom, double-click to reset, click a legend row to hide a series. Moving the pointer reads every series at that n. The dashed grey curves are O(n) and O(n log n) references anchored at logos's largest measured point, so a series that bends above the O(n log n) curve is growing faster than a comparison sort should. The label after each series name is its empirical exponent, the least-squares slope of log t against log n over n ≥ 100k (below that fixed costs dominate). The single-threaded panel compares sorts that each use one thread; the parallel panel compares sorts that each use all threads. Every output was checked equal to std::sort's on the same data; the minimum of the repetitions is plotted. A line that stops early hit the per-sort time budget.
