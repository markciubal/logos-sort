Treemap area is thread-time (threads × wall). "Working" is CPU time the threads actually received inside a phase; "preempted" is wall time inside a phase that the OS gave to another process (a loaded machine shows up here, not as engine cost); "stalled" is time blocked at a barrier waiting for the slowest thread; "idle" is time after a thread ran out of work (or, on serial phases, the other threads). GB/s uses the engine's known bytes per phase (histogram 9n, scatter 17n, copyback 16n, relocation probe 8n, relocation move 32n) against the memcpy calibration; a streaming phase far below that bandwidth is latency-bound. Every output verified against std::sort; best of 3 per cell.
